Piet Mondrian

A Dutch painter and art theoretician, who is regarded as one of the greatest artists of the 20th century.

Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow is an oil on canvas painting by Piet Mondrian, from 1930.

1872 - Born in Amersfoort, the Netherlands

1911 - Moves to Paris and sees Cubism

1917 - Co-founds De Stijl with Theo van Doesburg

1930 - Paints Composition with Red, Blue and Yellow

1944 - Dies in New York
